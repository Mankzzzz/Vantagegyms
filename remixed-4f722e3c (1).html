Our Process

Seven stages. No surprises.

Every Vantage build follows the same rigorous 7 stage process, from your first conversation through to the day you train in your new gym.

1
Discovery Consultation
Free in home visit. We assess your space, understand your goals, and scope the project. No obligation.
1 to 2 weeks
2
Design and Equipment Curation
3D renderings, mood boards, equipment selection from Canadian available inventory, and material specifications.
2 to 3 weeks
3
Fixed Price Proposal
Detailed written quote, timeline, and subcontractor agreements. Everything in writing. 30% deposit at signing.
1 week
4
Pre Build Coordination
Permits filed where required, equipment ordered, trades scheduled, and all logistics confirmed.
2 to 4 weeks
5
Construction
Flooring, walls, electrical (BC Hydro compliant), HVAC, acoustics, and smart system rough in. Daily supervision.
3 to 8 weeks
6
Equipment Installation
Delivery, assembly, calibration, AV setup, and full technology testing. Everything working before we leave.
1 to 2 weeks
7
Handover
Full walkthrough, equipment training session, documentation package, warranty activation, and maintenance plan.
1 to 3 days

Our Story

A gym should fit you

Vantage Gyms started with a simple frustration: every gym looks the same. The same machines in the same rows, the same layout, the same experience, regardless of who you are or how you train.

Sarthak noticed it first. Five years of serious training taught him that the best workout comes from a space designed around you. Your goals, your body, your rhythm. No commercial gym could offer that. But a home gym could.

Ibraheem saw the opportunity. With a sharp eye for brand, experience, and what people actually want, he understood that luxury homeowners weren't just buying equipment. They were buying a lifestyle statement and a daily ritual.

Together, they built Vantage to be the company that finally bridges the gap: exceptional design, premium equipment, and a build process that treats your home with the respect it deserves.

"Every gym we build is different. Because every client is different. That's the whole point."
Sarthak and Ibraheem, Co Founders
